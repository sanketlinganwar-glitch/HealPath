About Sanjivani Hospital & HealPath

Dedicated to bringing modern, compassionate healthcare and queue-free clinical appointments to patients in Yavatmal and surrounding districts.

OUR FOUNDING MISSION

Combining Experienced Medical Minds with Intelligent Queue Care

At Sanjeevan Multispecialty Hospital & Research Institute (Yavatmal 445001), we believe that medical care should be dignified, transparent, and prompt. Hospital visits often burden anxious patients and their families with unpredictable waiting hours in crowded corridors.

HealPath was built to eliminate this bottleneck: delivering precision slot booking, real-time live queue tracking via WebSockets, and instant WhatsApp updates. Patients know exactly when their turn approaches, arriving at the hospital at the right time.

Zero Crowded Lobbies
