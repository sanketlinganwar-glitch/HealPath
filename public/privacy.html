Hospital Privacy & Patient Data Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Sanjeevan Multispecialty Hospital & Research Institute, Yavatmal (445001).

1. Commitment to Patient Confidentiality

Sanjeevan Multispecialty Hospital & Research Institute ("Sanjivani Hospital", "we", "our") values the trust you place in us when sharing your personal and medical information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, safeguard, and disclose patient information through our digital appointment portal, live queue boards, and clinical consultations.

All clinical and identifying records are collected in strict compliance with applicable Indian medical ethics codes, clinical establishment guidelines, and healthcare data security protocols.

2. Information We Collect

When booking an appointment or registering at reception, we collect:

  • Patient Demographics: Full name, age, gender, contact address.
  • Communication Details: Mobile number, WhatsApp contact number for receiving token slips and slot reminders.
  • Consultation Context: Primary reason for visit, symptoms noted for the consulting physician, selected department and doctor.
  • Digital Check-in Credentials: Encrypted appointment tokens and QR check-in codes generated for verified hospital arrival.

3. Purpose of Processing

We use your data solely for:

  • Scheduling and confirming outpatient (OPD) consultation slots with our medical specialists.
  • Displaying anonymized live token indicators (e.g., token number and room number) on hospital waiting room monitors.
  • Dispatching automated SMS or WhatsApp notifications regarding booking confirmations, 10-minute auto-shifts, and arrival check-ins.
  • Internal hospital administration, quality assurance, and compliance with statutory medical record retention requirements.

4. WhatsApp & Communication Consent

By entering your WhatsApp number in the HealPath slot booking system, you consent to receive clinical scheduling messages, QR check-in passes, and live queue status updates. We do not use your WhatsApp contact for unsolicited commercial advertising or share it with third-party marketing brokers.

5. Data Security and Access Controls

Access to patient records is strictly restricted to authorized medical officers, consulting physicians, and reception staff bound by professional confidentiality. All network transmissions use secure SSL/TLS encryption.
